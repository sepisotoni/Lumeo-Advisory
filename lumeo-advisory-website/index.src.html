How it works

A simple monthly rhythm, so nothing piles up.

  1. Intro call

    Twenty minutes on your business, your software and where the records stand today.

  2. Connect and share

    You share bank statements, invoices and receipts as Excel or PDF files through an agreed method. You do not need to connect bank feeds or give us access to your apps.

  3. We close the month

    We capture, reconcile and query anything unclear within ten working days of month-end.

  4. You get your numbers

    Your monthly report arrives, with a short call whenever something needs a decision.

Questions we hear often

Do you provide both accounting and bookkeeping?

Yes. Lumeo provides accounting and bookkeeping services, including monthly records, reconciliations, reports, VAT and payroll admin, and year-end support.

Which accounting software do you use?

We mainly use Xero. If your records are elsewhere, talk to us about sharing exports as Excel or PDF files.

Do you need access to my accounting app or bank feeds?

No. You can send us your statements, reports and supporting records as Excel or PDF files for us to prepare your accounts and filing. You do not need to connect bank feeds or share your app login.

What records should I send?

Usually bank statements, sales and purchase invoices, receipts, payroll records and any relevant reports. We will confirm what is needed for your business and filing.

How do I send you my documents?

We agree on a way to share them, such as email or a shared folder. Excel and PDF files are fine; you do not need to send passwords or app logins.

My records are months behind. Can you still help?

Yes. A catch-up project comes first, then you can move onto a monthly plan.

Do you work with small retailers?

Yes. We support small retailers, shops and online stores, as well as other owner-run businesses.
